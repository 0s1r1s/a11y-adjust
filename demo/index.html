Long-form content is a good test for reading aids. Try the reading guide or the reading mask while scrolling through this article, and increase the line height or letter spacing to see how the text reflows.

The workshop

Our workshop sits at the edge of a small harbor town. Every morning starts with the same ritual: coffee, a look at yesterday's kiln results, and a short walk along the pier. The rhythm of the tides has shaped how we think about production. Nothing is rushed, and every piece is inspected twice before it leaves the building.

We source clay from a family-run quarry two hours inland. The linen comes from a weaving mill that has operated since 1911. Wood is bought only from certified forests, and offcuts become coasters, spoons and firewood for the winter.

"Good things take the time they need."

Care guide

  • Wash ceramics by hand to keep the glaze bright.
  • Linen softens with every wash. Avoid fabric softener.
  • Oil wooden boards once a month with food-safe oil.

A note on packaging

All packaging is plastic-free and recyclable. We reuse shipping boxes from suppliers whenever possible, which is why your parcel might have a surprising logo on it.
